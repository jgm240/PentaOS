# PentaOS Branding Guide

## Logo Overview
The PentaOS logo features a pentagon divided into 5 colorful sections, each representing a key pillar of the operating system.

## Color Palette

| Color | Hex Code | RGB | Purpose |
|-------|----------|-----|---------|
| Red | #FF6B6B | (255, 107, 107) | Innovation & Performance |
| Cyan | #4ECDC4 | (78, 205, 196) | Connectivity & Communication |
| Green | #95E77D | (149, 231, 125) | Sustainability & Growth |
| Purple | #A78BFA | (167, 139, 250) | Creativity & Customization |
| Orange | #FFB84D | (255, 184, 77) | Energy & Reliability |

## Logo Variants

### Full Logo
- Location: `pentaos-logo.svg`
- Best for: Official documentation, headers, branding materials
- Background: Dark background recommended (#1a1a2e)

### Simplified Icon
- Location: `pentaos-icon-simple.svg`
- Best for: Favicons, taskbars, small UI elements
- Size: 16x16 to 256x256 pixels

## Typography
- Primary Font: Arial, Helvetica (sans-serif)
- Logo Text: "PentaOS"
- Tagline: "Multi-core Excellence for Raspberry Pi"

## Usage Guidelines
1. Always maintain clear space around the logo (minimum 20px)
2. Do not rotate or distort the pentagon
3. Minimum logo size: 64x64 pixels
4. Use the color palette consistently across all materials
5. Maintain the dark background for optimal contrast
